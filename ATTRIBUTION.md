# Attribution

Bubblegum was originally developed in [Omarchy Kids](https://github.com/peterholko/omarchy-kids/tree/main/themes/bubblegum), maintained by Peter Holko. Its palette, six wallpapers, keyboard colour, icon preference, screensaver, and unlock artwork are carried over unchanged here.

The Omarchy wordmark and related inherited material come from [Omarchy](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy). Its MIT copyright notice is preserved alongside Peter Holko's notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The desktop preview is an unchanged frame from Peter Holko's Omarchy Kids demonstration recording. It shows the included Spring Meadow wallpaper. The extra desktop widgets visible in that recording belong to the Kids installation.

`icons.theme` selects the installed Yaru-magenta icon set; this repository does not bundle the Yaru icons. Omarchy generates application theme configurations from `colors.toml`.
